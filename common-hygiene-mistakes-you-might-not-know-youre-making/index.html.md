# You’re Probably Doing These Daily Hygiene Habits Wrong \(And How to Fix Them\)

We all like to keep it 100 – if we take care of ourselves and keep up our personal hygiene, we’ll feel the best we can too. You might be surprised that there are simple, everyday mistakes you can make when it comes to staying clean and fresh. Luckily, we have them all right here for you so you can avoid them and keep your routine on point!

## 1 Sharing Makeup

Okay, I know what you’re thinking: what could be so bad about borrowing that tube of lipstick or a quick swipe of mascara from your BFF? The answer is simple – germs. Sharing cosmetics transfers bacteria, oil, and dead skin cells directly between people. It’s not worth ending up with pink eye or a bacterial infection from sharing makeup, so bring your own products and avoid the risk altogether. According to cosmetic safety guidelines from the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org), sharing eye makeup in particular significantly increases the danger of spreading eye infections.

## 2 Post-Gym Showering

Sometimes you might feel like showering after a workout is a pain, especially when you are pressed for time. Maybe you have somewhere to go or errands to run, but it is essential to make time for a rinse after exercising. Sweat trapped in sweaty gym clothes can trap bacteria against your skin, causing acne breakouts and skin irritation. I always make sure to wash off the sweat and bacteria right after working out. Plus, you’ll feel refreshed, smell great, and maintain skin clarity. Keeping up with proper body care post-workout is just as vital as daily skin [moisturizing](#5).

- Rinse off within 30 minutes of finishing a workout.
- Change out of sweaty activewear immediately.
- Use a clean towel to pat skin dry without harsh rubbing.

## 3 Skipping Flossing

I hate flossing. And you probably do too. But it actually is super important, just like your dentist always says! Even if you brush thoroughly twice a day, toothbrush bristles cannot reach the tight spaces between your teeth. Flossing helps to keep your gums in good working order by removing food particles and reducing the bacteria trapped there. Nobody wants gingivitis or persistent bad breath, so make flossing a non-negotiable part of your daily oral care routine. As recommended by the [American Dental Association](https://www.ada.org), cleaning between your teeth daily is key to preventing gum disease.

## 4 Shampooing Daily

It seems like it would be good to shampoo your hair every single day, but the opposite is actually true. Over-washing strips your scalp of its natural protective oils, which can cause your glands to overcompensate by producing even more oil. If you shampoo less often, your scalp balances out and your hair stays cleaner longer. If your hair gets greasy easily like mine, you can use a quality dry shampoo between washes to refresh your roots and absorb excess oil without drying out your strands. Paired with [gentle, natural soaps](#6) in the shower, reducing shampoo frequency keeps your hair and scalp healthier.

## 5 Moisturizing

A lot of us with acne-prone skin tend to think that adding moisture to the skin will make breakouts worse. It seems crazy, but all skin types—including oily and acne-prone skin—need to be hydrated to stay healthy, maintain the natural barrier, and heal effectively. Skipping moisturizer can cause your skin to overproduce sebum to compensate for dryness, leading to more clogged pores. Don’t skip that step in your beauty routine! Always balance your cleansers and exfoliants with a light, nourishing moisturizer to put your best face forward. For more on skin barrier repair and hydration techniques, resources on [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com) emphasize the importance of non-comedogenic formulas.

| Hygiene Area | Common Mistake | Recommended Habit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skincare | Skipping moisturizer when acne-prone | Use non-comedogenic hydrator daily |
| Dental Care | Brushing only, skipping floss | Floss at least once per day |
| Electronics | Never sanitizing phone screen | Wipe down daily with disinfectant |

## 6 Using Irritating Soaps

The soap you use is actually more important than you’d think. Harsh chemical cleansers can strip your skin’s natural lipid barrier, causing redness, dryness, and irritation. Finding natural, gentle products to use in the shower can make a massive difference for sensitive skin. Look for nourishing ingredients like shea butter, cocoa butter, and calming essential oils when shopping for body washes and bar soaps. They moisturize and hydrate while effectively cleansing the skin.

## 7 Not Cleaning Electronics

We touch our smartphones, tablets, and computer keyboards constantly throughout the day, but do we clean them as often as we should? Making sure to clean frequently used devices like TV remotes, phone screens, and keyboards removes the gross bacteria and oils living on the surfaces. Our phones are a major breeding ground for germs, and touching them before touching your face can transfer bacteria similar to [sharing makeup](#1). Sanitize your tech items daily using electronic-safe wipes, as advised in public health guidance by the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov).

These are just a few common hygiene mistakes that we’ve probably all made at one time or another. By making a few small tweaks to your daily routine, you can keep your skin, teeth, and environment feeling fresh and healthy. Are there any hygiene tips or habits that I missed?
