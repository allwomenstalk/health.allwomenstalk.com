# Does the Claw Clip Migraine Hack Work? What Doctors Say

There are no studies showing that a hair claw clip on your eyebrow treats migraine. Doctors think pressure near a forehead nerve might briefly distract from pain for some people, but that is a theory, not a treatment. A clip near the eye can also irritate skin or make pain worse. If migraines keep coming back, ask a clinician about proven options.

## What is the claw clip migraine hack?

Videos show people placing a hair clip across the eyebrow on the side of a headache and reporting relief. It makes sense to be curious when a migraine is miserable. But personal reports cannot tell us whether the clip relieved a migraine, another kind of headache, or neither.

## Does it actually work?

**Verdict: unproven.** The doctors interviewed by [Prevention](https://www.prevention.com/health/a74034790/tiktok-claw-clip-migraine-hack/) said there are no studies of this particular hack. Stephen Thorp, MD, suggested that pressure over the supraorbital nerve, a branch of the trigeminal nerve, could compete with pain signals. He explicitly described that explanation as simplified. Neurologist Clifford Segil, DO, compared the effect to one sensation distracting from another. Neither theory shows that a clip stops a migraine attack.

Headache specialist Sana Ghafoor, MD, also told Prevention that the clip is untested and does not address migraine’s multiple causes. Any relief someone feels may be brief, and a clip is no substitute for their usual migraine care.

### Is it like an electrical nerve-stimulation device?

No. In a [randomized trial involving 67 people with episodic migraine](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23390177/), a purpose-built device delivered controlled electrical stimulation to the supraorbital area for **prevention**. The active treatment group improved more than the sham group on monthly migraine measures. That finding concerns electrical stimulation under a study protocol; it cannot be transferred to a mechanical hair clip, and it does not show that a clip can stop an attack.

## Is the clip safe near your eye?

The risks are small but real. The neurologists quoted by Prevention warned about [skin irritation, bruising, tenderness, worse pain and numbness or tingling from too much pressure](https://www.prevention.com/health/a74034790/tiktok-claw-clip-migraine-hack/). Segil also raised concern that a clip could interfere with closing the eye normally. If you have tried it and feel sharp pain, tingling or numbness, remove it. Keep any clip away from the eye itself.

## What can actually help a migraine?

[MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/migraine.html) describes migraine care as a mix of avoiding known triggers, treating an attack and, for some people, preventive treatment. During an attack, resting in a quiet, dark room, drinking fluids and using a cool cloth may help some people. A clinician or pharmacist can help you choose an appropriate over-the-counter medicine; prescription options include migraine-specific medicines. Medication choice depends on your health history, other medicines and how often attacks occur.

Keep a simple record of attacks, symptoms, possible triggers and medicines used. That gives a clinician a clearer picture and can help avoid relying on a viral trick while a treatable pattern continues. For more background on options, see our [migraine treatment guide](https://health.allwomenstalk.com/do-you-have-migraines-this-will-help/).

## When should you get medical help?

Make a routine appointment if headaches are frequent, changing, or not responding to your usual plan. A clinician can confirm the diagnosis and discuss treatment or prevention.

**Get urgent medical help** for a sudden severe headache, or a headache with new weakness, trouble speaking, vision changes, confusion, fever or a stiff neck. [MedlinePlus lists these as headache danger signs](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000424.htm). Do not assume a new or unusual severe headache is your usual migraine.

## Claw clip migraine hack FAQ

### Does a claw clip on the eyebrow help migraine?

There is no study showing that it treats migraine. A few people report temporary relief, but the effect and the reason for it have not been tested.

### Is the hack safe?

A clip can irritate or bruise skin, worsen pain or cause tingling, and it sits close to a vulnerable eye. Remove it if it hurts or causes unusual sensations.

### Which nerve might it press on?

Doctors have proposed the supraorbital nerve, which supplies feeling to part of the forehead. That proposed mechanism has not been tested for this hack.

### Are medical nerve devices the same thing?

No. The research cited above tested controlled electrical stimulation for migraine prevention. A hair clip applies mechanical pressure and has no comparable evidence.

### What helps a migraine attack?

Rest in a quiet, dark place, fluids and a cool cloth can help some people. Ask a clinician about suitable over-the-counter and prescription medicines, especially if attacks are recurring.

### When should I see a doctor?

Seek routine care for frequent or changing headaches or a plan that no longer helps. Seek urgent care for the danger signs above.
