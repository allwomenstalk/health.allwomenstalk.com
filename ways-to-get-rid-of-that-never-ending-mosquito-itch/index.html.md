# 7 Easy Ways to Stop That Never\-Ending Mosquito Itch Fast

Summer would be the perfect time of the year if it stayed focused on warm weather and outdoor fun, without the pesky mosquitoes that ruin all the excitement. Seriously, you can’t escape them; you’re not even safe in your own backyard or home! Therefore, since avoiding nature’s inexplicably evil creations is fruitless, the best option is to soothe your mosquito bites as soon as you get them. Here is a list of simple ways you can relieve your itch and get rid of mosquito bites fast!

Before applying any topical remedy to your skin, keep these essential safety tips in mind:

- Wash the affected area gently with mild soap and cool water first.
- Avoid aggressive scratching to prevent scarring and secondary bacterial infections.
- Perform a quick patch test on a small patch of skin when trying new ingredients.

## 1. Apply Apple Cider Vinegar on Affected Areas

A really quick way to stop that never-ending mosquito itch is simply by dabbing diluted [apple cider vinegar](https://www.bragg.com) over the infected area. Putting vinegar on mosquito bites works effectively because apple cider vinegar is less acidic in comparison to distilled white vinegar, helping restore the natural pH balance of your skin. As a result, this balance in pH effectively helps soothe the burning urge to scratch those pesky bumps!

## 2. Use Tea Bags to Reduce Itching

Don’t throw away your used tea bag right after finishing your tea. Instead, apply it directly onto the mosquito bite to reduce inflammation! Leave it there for about five minutes and experience instant relief. Tea contains natural tannins, antibacterial properties, and anti-inflammatory compounds along with being astringent, which helps accelerate the overall healing process. While some people swear by the [mosquito bite spoon trick](#3) for temporary thermal relief, cold tea bags provide a gentle, natural soothing alternative.

## 3. Rub Wet Aspirin on the Mosquito Bites

Grab an [aspirin](https://www.bayer.com) from your medicine cabinet for a quick and easy way to end your itchy frustration! Just wet the affected area and gently rub a crushed aspirin tablet over the spot. Aspirin contains acetylsalicylic acid, a strong anti-inflammatory compound that will control the swelling and redness caused by the bite in no time. Just make sure you aren’t allergic to aspirin medication first to avoid the need for medical attention.

## 4. Dab Toothpaste Where Needed

There is no need to rummage through your kitchen to reduce your itching because there is an easy fix using a bathroom essential! You can use toothpaste, preferably peppermint flavored, to shield the mosquito bite from bacteria and additionally relieve the maddening urge to itch. Just dab a small amount on and leave it to do its job for about 5-10 minutes. If you have ever wondered does deodorant stop mosquito bites from itching, antiperspirants containing aluminum salts can also temporarily dry out the bite and reduce swelling in a pinch.

## 5. Squeeze a Little Bit of Lemon Juice

Lemon juice has natural antibacterial and antimicrobial properties, which help to naturally alleviate itching and cleanse the skin surrounding insect bites. However, if you do choose this method, make sure to stay indoors upon application since exposure to sunlight can lead to phytophotodermatitis, causing blistering or skin staining. Getting rid of one problem by facing another is never a good idea!

## 6. Use Your Fingernail to Press an “X”

When none of the products listed above are available to you, your best bet is to use the classic "X" technique. Use your fingernails to press an "X" into your bite and feel the instant, but unfortunately temporary, itch relief. This technique temporarily disrupts the pain and itch signals sent to your brain while dispersing localized histamine pressure over the bite. However, as with applying [diluted apple cider vinegar](#1), it is best to wash your hands first to avoid pushing bacteria under your nails into the affected area.

## 7. Take a Warm Bath for Instant Relief

Taking a nice warm bath will not only be relaxing after a long day outdoors but also soothing for widespread insect bites across your body. For best results, add apple cider vinegar or two cups of colloidal oatmeal to the bath, and soak up the beneficial ingredients until you experience total comfort. The mix of warm water and your added product of choice should be enough to stop full-body itching. Similar to taking a [soothing oatmeal bath](#7), staying hydrated and keeping the skin moisturized promotes faster skin recovery.

Excessive itching of mosquito bites can damage the skin barrier, leading to scarring and bacterial infections. To combat these problems, try out one or several of these natural remedies to find something that works best for your skin!
